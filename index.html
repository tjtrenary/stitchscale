📖 How to Interface This Blueprint with Your Original Pattern:
  1. Stitch Definitions & Motifs: Keep your original pattern PDF open for special stitch keys, lace motifs, and ribbing rules.
  2. Starting Cast-On / Chain: Substitute the original pattern's starting stitch count with your Blueprint Cast-On Number listed below.
  3. Working Length & Shaping: Work in pattern until you reach the total rows or shaping intervals listed below, checking off row checkboxes as you go.
  4. Borders, Edgings & Seaming Conversion:
    • Top/Bottom Borders: Check that your rescaled stitch count matches any border stitch repeat (e.g. multiple of 4 + 1). Use the Stitch Repeat Guard if needed.
    • Side-Edge Pickup: When picking up stitches along row ends, use a standard ratio (e.g. 1 st per SC row end, 3 sts per 4 HDC/DC row ends) to prevent edge puckering or flaring.
    • Seaming: If both panels were rescaled (e.g., Front & Back), seam row-for-row as instructed in the original pattern.

📏 Need help swatching & measuring non-rectangular shapes?
  1. Flat Swatch First: Swatch a flat 5" x 5" square in your main stitch pattern (even for sleeves or circles) to measure baseline gauge per 4 inches / 10 cm.
  2. Sleeves & Tapers: Measure your cuff diameter and fullest bicep width. StitchScale calculates the exact shaping interval automatically.
  3. Circles & Crowns: Measure target circumference or flat crown diameter across the center before transitioning to straight sides.
