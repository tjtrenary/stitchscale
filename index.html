📏 Need help swatching & measuring non-rectangular shapes?
  1. Flat Swatch First: Swatch a flat 5" x 5" square in your main stitch pattern (even for sleeves or circles) to measure baseline gauge per 4 inches / 10 cm.
  2. Sleeves & Tapers: Measure your cuff diameter and fullest bicep width. StitchScale calculates the exact shaping interval automatically.
  3. Circles & Crowns: Measure target circumference or flat crown diameter across the center before transitioning to straight sides.
