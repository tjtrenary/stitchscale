📏 Need help swatching & measuring accurately?
  1. Work a Swatch: Cast on enough stitches to measure at least 5 to 6 inches (12–15 cm) square in your chosen yarn and hook/needle size.
  2. Block Your Fabric: Lay your swatch flat without stretching or pulling it out of shape.
  3. Measure in the Center: Place a ruler across the middle. Count how many stitches fit inside exactly 4 inches (10 cm). Do the same vertically for row count.
